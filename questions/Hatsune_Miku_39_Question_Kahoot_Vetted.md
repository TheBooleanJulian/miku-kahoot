Q1 • EASY — SINGLE SELECT
Which company created and released Hatsune Miku?
A. SEGA
B. Internet Co., Ltd.
C. Yamaha Music
D. Crypton Future Media
Answer: D
Explanation: Crypton Future Media (Sapporo) developed and released Miku as part of their Character Vocal Series. SEGA publishes the Project DIVA games; Yamaha created the VOCALOID engine itself.

Q2 • EASY — SINGLE SELECT
What is Hatsune Miku primarily?
A. A rhythm-game mascot created by SEGA
B. A human singer whose stage name is Miku
C. A virtual singer and singing voicebank character
D. A fictional idol who has never had a voicebank
Answer: C
Explanation: Miku is a voicebank for Yamaha's VOCALOID (and later NT) synthesis software, marketed as a 'virtual singer.' She is not a human performer, nor is she limited to games.

Q3 • EASY — SINGLE SELECT
When was the original Japanese Hatsune Miku voicebank released?
A. 2008
B. 2007
C. 2006
D. 2010
Answer: B
Explanation: The Hatsune Miku VOCALOID2 voicebank launched on 31 August 2007.

Q4 • EASY — SINGLE SELECT
Who illustrated Miku's original character design?

B. Mogumo
C. redjuice
D. KEI
Answer: D
Explanation: KEI designed the original box art and character illustration. iXima, Mogumo, and redjuice are well-known Vocaloid artists but did not create Miku's default design.

Q5 • EASY — SINGLE SELECT
Whose voice was used as the basis for Miku's original Japanese voicebank?
A. Ayane Sakura
B. Saki Fujita
C. Kana Hanazawa
D. Aoi Yūki

Explanation: Voice actress Saki Fujita provided the sampled phonemes. Crypton processed her recordings into the VOCALOID2 library.

Q9 • EASY — SINGLE SELECT
What is the famous Japanese wordplay behind the number 39?
A. san-kyuu ≈ 'thank you'
B. san-ku ≈ 'three-nine'
C. mi-ku ≈ 'future song'
D. miku ≈ 'three songs'
Answer: A
Explanation: San-kyuu (39) sounds like 'thank you' in Japanese. It is also read as mi-ku, which is why March 9 (3/9) is celebrated as 'Miku Day.'

Explanation: The Project DIVA series (SEGA, 2009–present) is built around Miku as the central icon. Project SEKAI features her alongside original human characters, but DIVA is the classic Miku-centric rhythm franchise.

Q11 • MEDIUM — SINGLE SELECT
In Vocaloid culture, what does 'producer' usually mean?
A. A person who manufactures the voicebank hardware

Answer: D
Explanation: A Vocaloid 'producer' is any musician/composer who writes, arranges, and tunes songs using Vocaloid/UTAU/SynthV voicebanks.

Q12 • MEDIUM — SINGLE SELECT
What is a singing voicebank?
A. A collection of official concert recordings
B. A database containing every Vocaloid song
C. Recorded vocal material and data used by synthesis software
D. A library of character illustrations
Answer: C
Explanation: A voicebank is a library of recorded phonemes (and, in modern engines, AI-trained vocal data) that the synthesis engine stitches into singing.
